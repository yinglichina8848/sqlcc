详细描述

The primary execution engine, acting as the "Context" in the Strategy pattern.

This class orchestrates the entire execution process. It does not know how to execute any specific statement; instead, it holds a collection of ExecutionStrategy objects and delegates the work to the correct one based on the parsed statement's type.
