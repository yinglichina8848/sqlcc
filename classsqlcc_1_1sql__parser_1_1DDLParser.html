DDLParser - 数据定义语言解析器

负责解析SQL的DDL语句：

  • CREATE TABLE/DATABASE/INDEX/USER/PROCEDURE/TRIGGER/VIEW
  • ALTER TABLE
  • DROP TABLE/DATABASE/INDEX/USER/PROCEDURE/TRIGGER/VIEW
